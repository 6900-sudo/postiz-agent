## Voiceover + on-screen text

**Beat 1 — Hook (7.5s)**
- VO: "The NHS isn't being cut. It's being sold. And the 2025 Ten Year Health Plan says the quiet part out loud."
- Screen: *NHS PRIVATISATION* — "The NHS isn't being CUT. It's being **SOLD**."

**Beat 2 — The Plan / Milburn (6.8s)**
- VO: "It openly welcomes private partnership. Alan Milburn now calls the NHS an ecosystem of private providers."
- Screen: *THE 2025 10-YEAR HEALTH PLAN* — "Openly welcomes private 'partnership'." + quote card: "Milburn calls the NHS an 'ecosystem' of private providers."

**Beat 3 — Follow the money (9.7s)**
- VO: "Follow the money. Private cataract firms extract thirty-two percent in profit. And sixty-eight million pounds in interest on NHS debt is paid straight to private equity."
- Screen: *FOLLOW THE MONEY* — **32%** profits extracted by private cataract firms · **£68m** interest on debt → private equity.

**Beat 4 — Cherry-picking / inequality (11.2s)**
- VO: "The private sector cherry-picks the easy, low-risk cases, and dumps the complex ones back on a depleted NHS. The gap in surgery access between the richest and poorest keeps widening."
- Screen: *CHERRY-PICKING* — "Private takes the easy, low-risk cases. Complex cases dumped on a depleted NHS. The surgery-access gap between richest and poorest keeps widening."

**Beat 5 — Safety / rescued (9.8s)**
- VO: "And when private care goes wrong? The NHS picks up the pieces. Sixty-six hundred private patients rescued every single year. Costing eighty million pounds."
- Screen: *WHEN PRIVATE CARE FAILS* — **6,600** private patients rescued by the NHS every year · **£80m** annual bill picked up by the public.

**Beat 6 — Call to action (4.4s)**
- VO: "Public money. Private profit. It's time to keep our NHS public."
- Screen: "PUBLIC MONEY. PRIVATE PROFIT." → **KEEP OUR NHS PUBLIC** → "Follow. Share. Defend it."

## Source facts (Keep Our NHS Public brief)

- **10 Year Health Plan (2025):** Actively welcomes private 'partnership'. Milburn describes the NHS as an 'ecosystem' of private providers.
- **Financial drain:** 32% of profits extracted by private cataract companies; £68m interest on debt paid to private equity.
- **Inequality:** Private sector cherry-picks low-risk cases, leaving complex cases to a depleted NHS. Surgery-access gap between richest and poorest widening.
- **Safety:** 6,600 private patients 'rescued' by the NHS annually, costing £80m.
